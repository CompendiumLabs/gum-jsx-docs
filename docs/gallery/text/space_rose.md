# Space Rose

*Category*: geometry

A glowing, weathered light-box sign floats in a seeded starfield, with a rose printed on its front face.

Ported from the old Gum gallery.

The traced sign geometry, layered halo, petal curves, dust, scuffs, and glints come from the old example. Local pixel-sized circles replace legacy radius/position props. The rose is an explicitly sized rotated group, and custom shades are approximated with the shared palette. The texture is deterministic.

See [Rotate](../../elements/text/Rotate.md) and [the runnable source](../code/space_rose.jsx).
