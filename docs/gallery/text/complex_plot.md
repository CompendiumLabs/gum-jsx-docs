# Complex Roots

*Category*: plotting

The real and imaginary components of the roots of x² + 2cx + 1 are plotted horizontally against the parameter c.

Ported from the old Gum gallery.

The real branches are sampled separately outside |c| = 1, and the imaginary components inside it. This avoids the old clamp creating artificial branches outside their domains. Upright math and text annotations use data positions and pixel font sizes.

See [SymLine](../../elements/text/SymLine.md) and [the runnable source](../code/complex_plot.jsx).
