# The Scenic Route

*Category*: math

A direct arrow, a hooked arrow, and a custom figure-eight arrow provide three routes from A to B.

Ported from the old Gum gallery.

The custom arrow occupies a MathBox with relation spacing. Its graph has explicit em dimensions, its spline has fixed head geometry, and the polar samples are converted from point objects to tuples for the path arithmetic. The old arrow-arc option has no counterpart; head-curve supplies the curved barb.

See [MathBox](../../elements/text/MathBox.md) and [the runnable source](../code/scenic_route.jsx).
