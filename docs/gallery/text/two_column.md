# Two Columns

*Category*: layout

A damped-oscillation plot sits beside a paragraph and a short list containing inline math.

Ported from the old Gum gallery.

The figure and prose receive explicit widths. SymLine samples both envelopes and the signal over the entire domain; text wraps at its allocation without shrinking its font.
